Common questions

Things you might be wondering

Is this treatment surgical?

No. Body Slimming is a non-surgical aesthetic clinic treatment, planned around your goals and delivered by our team with comfort-focused care.

How long does a session take?

Most sessions are scheduled for around 45 to 75 minutes. We confirm exact timing during your consultation, based on the treatment plan that suits you.

When can I return to daily activities?

Most clients return to their day right after the session. Your therapist will share simple aftercare guidance to support your results.

How do I know if this is right for me?

We start with a friendly consultation. Our team will review your goals and recommend whether Body Slimming or a different treatment is the best fit.
